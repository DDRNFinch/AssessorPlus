# Assessor+ / Apprentice+ Review Transfer v1

Assessor+ V3.4 accepts pseudonymised Apprentice+ review snapshots by QR text or `.apreview` file.

## Apprentice+ -> Assessor+

Direction: `ApprenticePlusToAssessorPlus`

Required privacy rule: do not include learner name, date of birth, email, phone, address, employer contact details, evidence media, signatures, safeguarding or health information.

Supported fields include course, standardVersion, reviewPeriod, progressPercent, KSB totals, assignment totals, OTJ totals, Academy score, EPA status, learner reflection, previous targets and five suggested targets.

## Assessor+ -> Apprentice+

Direction: `AssessorPlusToApprenticePlus`

The return package includes review ID, dates, RAG, attendance summary, tutor/learner/employer comments, agreed targets, barriers/support and agreement notes.

The learner identity is selected locally in Assessor+ after import and is not required in the incoming snapshot.
